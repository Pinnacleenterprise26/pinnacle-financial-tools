Snowball vs. Avalanche

The snowball method pays off the smallest balance first regardless of interest rate, building momentum through quick wins. The avalanche method targets the highest-interest debt first, which minimizes total interest paid mathematically. Snowball tends to help with motivation; avalanche tends to save more money.

When Each Makes Sense

If staying motivated is the biggest challenge, snowball's early wins can help you stick with the plan. If the interest rate gap between debts is large, avalanche's savings can be substantial enough to outweigh the psychological benefit of quick wins.
