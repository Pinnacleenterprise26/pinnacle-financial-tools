How EMI Is Calculated

EMI stands for Equated Monthly Installment - a fixed payment covering both interest and principal, so the loan is fully paid off by the end of the term. Early payments are interest-heavy; later payments are principal-heavy, since interest is calculated on whatever balance remains.

Fixed vs. Reducing Balance Interest

Most personal and auto loans use reducing balance interest - charged only on the remaining balance, which shrinks each month. Flat-rate loans, by contrast, charge interest on the original amount for the whole term, costing meaningfully more for the same stated rate.

What Extra Payments Actually Do

Extra payments go straight toward reducing principal rather than being split like a regular payment. Because future interest is calculated on a smaller balance, even modest extra payments early on can meaningfully shorten the payoff timeline.

Comparing Loan Offers Properly

Always compare total repayment - principal plus all interest and fees - rather than just the monthly payment or headline rate, since a lower monthly payment often just means a longer term and more interest paid overall.
