About Pinnacle Financial Tools

Pinnacle Financial Tools was created to make everyday financial calculations easier to understand and easier to use. The site provides free tools for mortgages, loans, income, debt, savings, refinancing, and property-tax planning without requiring an account or subscription.

Pinnacle Financial Tools is operated as part of Pinnacle Enterprise Services. We do not sell loans, investments, credit cards, insurance, or personalized financial advice through these calculators.

What We Aim to Provide

Each calculator is built around a specific financial question and paired with educational content that explains the calculation, common tradeoffs, and important limitations. The goal is to help visitors compare scenarios and ask better questions before making financial decisions.

Independence and Advertising

The site may display advertising to help support free access to the calculators and educational material. Advertising does not change the mathematical result produced by a calculator. An advertisement should never be interpreted as an endorsement or recommendation by Pinnacle Financial Tools.
