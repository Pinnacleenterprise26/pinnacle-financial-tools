How Property Tax Assessments Work

Local governments assess a home's value - sometimes at full market value, sometimes at a percentage of it depending on local rules - then apply the local tax rate to that assessed value. This is why two homes of identical market value in different counties can have very different tax bills.

Why Rates Vary So Much by Location

Property tax rates fund local services like schools, police, and infrastructure, so rates vary significantly by city, county, and state depending on local budgets and funding needs. Reassessment - when a local government updates a home's assessed value, often after a sale or periodically - can also cause a tax bill to change even if the rate itself stays the same.
