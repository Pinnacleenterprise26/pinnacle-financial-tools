Why Minimum Payments Barely Touch the Balance

Minimum payments on credit cards are often calculated as a small percentage of the balance - commonly around 2%. Because so much of that payment goes to interest first, the principal barely shrinks, which is why minimum-payment-only balances can take a decade or more to clear and cost far more in total interest.

How Credit Card Interest Compounds

Credit card interest typically compounds daily based on your APR, then gets applied to your statement monthly. Carrying a balance means interest is calculated on an amount that includes previously unpaid interest, which is part of why credit card debt can grow quickly if only minimum payments are made.
