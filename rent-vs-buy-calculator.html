Hidden Costs of Homeownership

Buying involves more than the mortgage payment - closing costs, maintenance, property tax, and insurance all add up. This calculator focuses on mortgage interest versus rent paid and home appreciation, but real decisions should also weigh maintenance costs, which commonly run 1-2% of home value per year.

Opportunity Cost of a Down Payment

Money used for a down payment could otherwise be invested. Whether buying wins financially over renting often comes down to how long you stay - the longer you stay, the more the fixed costs of buying (like closing costs) get spread out and outweighed by building equity.
