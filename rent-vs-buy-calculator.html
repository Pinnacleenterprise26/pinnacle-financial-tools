How This Rent vs. Buy Comparison Works

This calculator compares the estimated costs that generally do not become home equity. On the buying side, that includes mortgage interest, property taxes, homeowners insurance, maintenance, HOA fees, purchase closing costs, and estimated selling costs. Estimated home appreciation is treated as a financial benefit that reduces the net cost of ownership.

Why the Down Payment Is Not Counted as a Lost Cost

A down payment reduces the mortgage balance and becomes part of your home equity. Principal paid through monthly mortgage payments also builds equity. For that reason, this calculator does not treat the down payment or mortgage principal as money that simply disappears.

Rent Can Change Over Time

The renting calculation allows you to estimate an annual rent increase rather than assuming rent remains unchanged for the entire comparison period. Adjust the rate based on your own expectations and local market conditions.

Use the Assumptions That Fit Your Situation

Property taxes, insurance, maintenance, closing costs, HOA fees, appreciation, selling costs, and rent increases can vary substantially. The default values are planning assumptions only and should be changed to match the property and market you are evaluating.

Planning estimate only. This calculator assumes the home is sold at the end of the selected period when estimating selling costs. It does not include tax deductions or credits, mortgage insurance, utilities, renter's insurance, financing of closing costs, investment returns that could have been earned on the down payment or other cash, or the time value of money. Results are estimates, not financial, tax, real estate, or lending advice.
