When Refinancing Actually Makes Sense

Refinancing typically makes sense when the new rate is meaningfully lower than your current rate and you plan to stay in the home past the break-even point - the number of months it takes for your monthly savings to cover the closing costs.

Rate vs. Term Tradeoffs

Refinancing into a shorter term at a lower rate can dramatically cut lifetime interest, even if the monthly payment doesn't drop much. Refinancing into a longer term can lower monthly payments but may reset the amortization clock, increasing total interest paid over time.
