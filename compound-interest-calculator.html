How Compounding Works

Compound interest means you earn returns not just on what you put in, but on the returns you've already earned. Over long periods, this creates accelerating growth - the balance grows faster in later years than in early ones, even with the same contribution amount.

Why Starting Early Matters More Than Contributing More Later

Money invested earlier has more time to compound, which is why a smaller amount invested young can outgrow a larger amount invested later. This calculator assumes monthly compounding on an average annual return, with monthly contributions added at the beginning of each month before that month's growth is applied. Real investment returns will vary and are not guaranteed.
