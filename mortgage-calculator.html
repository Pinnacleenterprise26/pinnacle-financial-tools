How This Mortgage Calculator Works

Your monthly mortgage payment is made up of more than just paying back what you borrowed. This calculator estimates your full monthly cost by combining four components, often remembered by the acronym PITI: Principal, Interest, Taxes, and Insurance. Principal is the portion that goes toward paying down the actual loan balance, while interest is the cost of borrowing, calculated on your remaining balance each month.

Why Your Rate Matters More Than You'd Think

A seemingly small difference in interest rate can add up to tens of thousands of dollars over a 30-year loan, since interest compounds on your remaining balance every month. This is why it's worth shopping multiple lenders rather than accepting the first rate offered.

Understanding Your Amortization Schedule

An amortization schedule breaks down exactly how much of each payment goes to principal versus interest, year by year. In the early years, interest dominates; as the balance shrinks, more of each payment shifts toward principal - which is why extra payments early in the loan can meaningfully shorten the term.

Common Mistakes When Estimating a Mortgage

A frequent mistake is looking only at principal-and-interest and forgetting taxes, insurance, and HOA fees. Another is not accounting for PMI, which typically applies when the down payment is under 20%. Always compare total interest paid, not just the monthly payment.
