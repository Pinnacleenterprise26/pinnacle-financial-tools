New vs. Used Loan Rates

New car loans typically carry lower interest rates than used car loans, since lenders see less risk in a vehicle with a known history and higher resale value. The gap can be a percentage point or more, so it's worth checking rates for both if you're deciding between them.

How Trade-In Value and Sales Tax Factor In

A trade-in reduces the amount you need to finance, and in many states it also reduces the taxable purchase price, meaning you pay sales tax only on the difference between the new vehicle's price and your trade-in's value - a meaningful savings depending on your state's rules.

Loan Term Tradeoffs

A longer loan term lowers your monthly payment but increases the total interest paid and extends the time you owe more than the car is worth. Shorter terms cost more per month but save significantly on total interest over the life of the loan.
